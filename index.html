Who I Am
About

Part-time Electrical and Electronic Engineering student at the University of Western Australia, based in Perth. My evenings tend to involve datasheets, a soldering iron, and at least one thing that isn't working yet.

I do the whole build myself — schematic capture and PCB layout in KiCad, enclosures in Fusion 360, and getting microcontrollers to do what I want on ESP32. I build things to understand how they actually work, not to end up with something I'm afraid to open.

Right now I'm on revision two of the RetroDS PCB — moving from a hand-soldered perfboard to a proper 4-layer board in KiCad, with the MAX98357A audio amp finally wired in.
